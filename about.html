About Poshtik Campus

Poshtik Campus is a student-focused food initiative that provides affordable and nutritious meals on campus.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make healthy food accessible to every student without compromising on taste or affordability.

What We Offer

We prepare traditional Telangana and Andhra-inspired dishes using nutritious ingredients such as millets, sprouts, vegetables and protein-rich foods.

Why Poshtik?

Fresh ingredients, student-friendly prices and nutritious meals make Poshtik Campus a convenient choice for students.
